jutsu — Privacy policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026.

Intended audience

jutsu is intended for users aged 16 and older. It is not directed at children under 16.

Data used by jutsu

jutsu is a single-player training game. It processes head position and orientation and, when permission is granted, hand-joint information on your device to position game content and recognize controls. jutsu does not save a movement history or send tracking data or battle state to a developer-operated server.

When you allow the microphone, jutsu listens for the voice commands “pause” and “resume” during a fight and between its rounds. It uses the device's speech recognition service and asks it to recognize offline only, on the device. jutsu receives only the recognized words, uses them to pause or resume the game, and does not record, save, log or send audio or words. The speech recognition service is provided by your device or its supplier and follows their privacy policy. If offline English recognition is not available, voice commands are off and the game is played with hand gestures. The microphone is requested at your first fight, after an in-app explanation, and is optional.

The app has no account system, advertising, in-app purchases, developer-operated analytics, or automatic crash-report upload service. The developer does not sell tracking data or battle state or transmit them to third parties.

Storage and deletion

Battle state, including health and the current round, is held in memory during the current app session and discarded when the app process ends. Two settings are stored only on your device: whether you have finished the tutorial, and your best clear time. They are deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data. App cloud backup is disabled. There is no developer-held game profile or account to delete.

Device and platform services

jutsu uses the device's spatial runtime for tracking and rendering. Device and operating-system services may process diagnostics according to your device settings and their suppliers' privacy policies. These services are separate from developer-operated collection. For Google's services, see the Google Privacy Policy and your device's privacy controls.

This policy website

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. This website hosting activity is separate from data processing within the jutsu app. For details, see the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Permissions and security

You can change head-tracking, hand-tracking and microphone permissions in device settings. Without the microphone permission, voice commands are off and the rest of the game works as usual. jutsu does not access GPS location, camera images, Cloud Anchors, or the Geospatial API, and it does not record or store audio. It does not persist movement logs or upload them automatically. Ordinary local error messages may be produced when assets fail to load.
