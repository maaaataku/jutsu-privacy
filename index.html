jutsu — Privacy policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026.

Intended audience

jutsu is intended for users aged 16 and older. It is not directed at children under 16.

Data used by jutsu

jutsu is a single-player training game. It processes head position and orientation and, when permission is granted, hand-joint information on your device to position game content and recognize controls. jutsu does not save a movement history or send tracking data or battle state to a developer-operated server.

When you allow the microphone, jutsu listens during a fight and between its rounds for the voice commands “pause” and “resume” and, after you have made the five seals of a chant, for the name of the technique. It uses the device's speech recognition service and asks it to recognize offline only, on the device. jutsu receives only the recognized words, uses them to pause or resume the game or to release the technique, and does not record, save, log or send audio or words. The speech recognition service is provided by your device or its supplier and follows their privacy policy. Speech is heard in English; on a device set to Japanese that has no offline English recognition it is heard in Japanese instead. If neither is available, voice is off and the game is played with hand gestures. The microphone is requested at your first fight, after an in-app explanation, and is optional.

jutsu has no account system of its own, no advertising, no developer-operated analytics and no automatic crash-report upload service. The developer does not sell tracking data or battle state or transmit them to third parties.

Google Play Games and rankings

Signing in with Google Play Games is optional; a device that already has a Play Games profile signs in by itself, as set in your Play Games settings. Google processes your Play Games profile and sign-in under the Google Privacy Policy. While you are signed in, the result of a ranked run (the clear time of a battle, or the score of a boss survival run) is sent to Google Play Games and shown with your Play Games name on its leaderboards. Your Play Games settings decide who can see it. The names of other players shown on the in-game ranking are displayed only and are not saved by the app. Without signing in, nothing is sent and your records stay on the device. You can delete your Play Games data for jutsu in your Google account's Play Games settings.

Purchases

jutsu may offer looks (colour variations only; they never change damage or speed) through Google Play. Google Play handles the payment; the app receives only which looks you own, and keeps that list on the device so that it works offline. At the date of this policy no look is on sale and the game can be played in full without any purchase.

Storage and deletion

Battle state, including health and the current round, is held in memory during the current app session and discarded when the app process ends. The following are stored only on your device: your practice progress, your settings (sound volume, difficulty, enemy movement and the selected look), your best records, scores waiting to be sent to Google Play Games, and the list of looks you own. They are deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data. App cloud backup is disabled. There is no developer-held game profile or account to delete.

Device and platform services

jutsu uses the device's spatial runtime for tracking and rendering. Device and operating-system services may process diagnostics according to your device settings and their suppliers' privacy policies. These services are separate from developer-operated collection. For Google's services, see the Google Privacy Policy and your device's privacy controls.

This policy website

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. This website hosting activity is separate from data processing within the jutsu app. For details, see the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Permissions and security

You can change head-tracking, hand-tracking and microphone permissions in device settings. Without the microphone permission, voice commands are off and the rest of the game works as usual. jutsu does not access GPS location, camera images, Cloud Anchors, or the Geospatial API, and it does not record or store audio. It does not persist movement logs or upload them automatically. Ordinary local error messages may be produced when assets fail to load.
