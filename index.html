jutsu — Privacy policy

Last updated: 16 September 2026.

Intended audience

jutsu is intended for users aged 16 and older. It is not directed at children under 16.

Data used by jutsu

jutsu is a single-player training game. It processes head position and orientation and, when permission is granted, hand-joint information on your device to position game content and recognize controls. jutsu does not save a movement history or send tracking data or battle state to a developer-operated server.

The app has no account system, advertising, in-app purchases, developer-operated analytics, or automatic crash-report upload service. The developer does not sell tracking data or battle state or transmit them to third parties.

Storage and deletion

Battle state, including health and the defeated count, is held in memory during the current app session. It is not saved as a persistent player profile or cloud save. This state is discarded when the app process ends. There is no developer-held game profile or account to delete. App backup and device-to-device transfer are disabled.

Device and platform services

jutsu uses the device's spatial runtime for tracking and rendering. Device and operating-system services may process diagnostics according to your device settings and their suppliers' privacy policies. These services are separate from developer-operated collection. For Google's services, see the Google Privacy Policy and your device's privacy controls.

This policy website

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. This website hosting activity is separate from data processing within the jutsu app. For details, see the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Permissions and security

You can change head- and hand-tracking permissions in device settings. On-screen command buttons remain available without jutsu's custom hand gestures. jutsu does not access GPS location, microphone recordings, camera images, Cloud Anchors, or the Geospatial API. It does not persist movement logs or upload them automatically. Ordinary local error messages may be produced when assets fail to load.

Contact

For questions about this policy or the handling of data in jutsu, contact: maaaataku.dev16@gmail.com.

If you send a support email, the developer will receive your email address and the contents you choose to send to respond to your inquiry. Please do not include passwords or unnecessary personal information. Support correspondence is kept only as needed to resolve the inquiry or meet applicable obligations; you can request its deletion through the same contact.
